headline: "What makes an intron stay? Reading intron retention from DNA sequence"
description: >
  Most introns are cut out of gene transcripts by splicing, but some are
  selectively retained, a form of alternative splicing that shapes gene
  regulation. In this study led by our PhD student Luca Faretra, we extracted
  exon-intron-exon units across the human genome and described each one with a
  broad set of sequence features, from splice-site strength and GC content to
  transposable elements and RNA-binding protein signatures. Machine learning
  models show that the tendency to be retained arises from the interplay of
  many factors, with intron length and GC content as the strongest predictors.
  Fully developed in our lab, in collaboration with Biogem.
