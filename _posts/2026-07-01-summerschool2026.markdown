  Our lab is taking part in the UniSannio Summer School "Scienze e Tecnologie",
  dedicated this year to crime scene investigation ("Prova a prendermi:
  dall'analisi delle tracce alla ricostruzione della dinamica del movimento
  sulla scena del crimine attraverso un approccio scientifico integrato").
  We are in charge of the closing session, "Biotech Crime Lab: il DNA non
  mente", on Thursday 16 July 2026 (9:30-12:30), covering the DNA analysis
  phase of the investigation. Students will get hands-on with the
  bioinformatics side of forensic genetics in our computer lab, seeing how
  DNA evidence collected on a crime scene is actually analyzed computationally
  rather than in a wet lab.
