headline: "Approved drugs that activate human brown fat cells"
description: >
  Brown adipose tissue burns energy to produce heat, making it an attractive
  target against obesity and metabolic disease. In this study, the DECCODE
  approach compared the transcriptional profile of human stem cell-derived
  brown adipocytes with drug-induced profiles from the LINCS database,
  searching for small molecules that promote their activation and
  differentiation. Two drugs already approved for other uses, saxagliptin and
  tolvaptan, significantly enhanced thermogenic activation in human cells,
  pointing to new drug-repurposing opportunities. A collaboration with the
  University of Cambridge, the Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics, CIPF
  Valencia and KAUST.
