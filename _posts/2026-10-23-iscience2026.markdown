headline: "Fewer centrosomes, more mature neurons: centrosome loss rewires RNA splicing"
description: >
  Centrosomes organize cell division, but they may also help decide what a
  cell becomes. In neuroblastoma stem cells, this study found splicing
  components such as the U2 and U5 snRNAs and the splicing factor SRSF2 at
  the centrosome's subdistal appendages. Removing centrosomes before
  retinoic acid treatment made the cells mature into neurons more
  efficiently, and this went together with more intron retention and with
  the dephosphorylation of SR splicing factors at the centrosome. Our lab
  carried out the bioinformatic analyses, led by Luca Faretra and Marco
  Nigro, in a study coordinated by Massimo Pancione with partners at the
  University of Naples Federico II, TIGEM and IRCCS Casa Sollievo della
  Sofferenza.
