headline: "Centrosome genes linked to pancreatic cancer outcome"
description: >
  Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma is among the leading causes of cancer
  death, and its cells often show abnormal centrosomes. Mining public
  RNA-sequencing data, this study identified two centrosome genes, CEP250
  and CEP170, whose high expression is associated with a worse prognosis, and
  confirmed the findings on patient tissues. In a small validation cohort,
  patients with high CEP250 levels had shorter survival and rarely responded
  to first-line chemotherapy, while low levels were associated with long-term
  survivors. A collaboration with the University of Foggia, Federico II
  University of Naples and Biogem.
