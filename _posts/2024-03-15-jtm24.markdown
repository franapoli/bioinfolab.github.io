headline: "New drug candidates and a risk score for osteoarthritis"
description: >
  Osteoarthritis affects a large share of middle-aged people, and current
  treatments are still poorly effective. Combining three patient cohorts, this
  study derived a consensus signature of 44 genes involved in the disease.
  Network-based drug prioritization pointed to candidates such as resveratrol,
  tenoxicam and mesalazine, and seven gene targets were confirmed in vitro.
  The authors also propose a risk score based on just eight genes that
  predicts the clinical stage of the disease from RNA-Seq data. A
  collaboration with Federico II University of Naples, Biogem, CNR, IRCCS
  SYNLAB SDN and the University of Miami.
