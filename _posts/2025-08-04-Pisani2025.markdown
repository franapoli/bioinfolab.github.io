headline: "Drugs that boost engineered cells, found computationally"
description: >
  Mammalian cells engineered with synthetic genetic circuits are at the heart
  of next-generation biotherapeutics, but their productivity is limited by
  finite cellular resources. Using DECCODE, a computational drug-matching
  method developed by Prof. Napolitano, the authors compared the
  transcriptional profile of cells carrying a performance-enhancing circuit
  with thousands of drug-induced profiles, looking for drugs that mimic its
  effect without further genetic changes. The top candidate, Filgotinib,
  increased the expression of genetic payloads across several cell lines and
  delivery systems, including AAV and lentivirus. A collaboration led by Velia
  Siciliano's lab at IIT Naples, with TIGEM, Federico II and CNR.
