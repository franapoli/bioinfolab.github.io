headline: "AI finds drugs that push breast cancer stem cells to differentiate"
description: >
  Cancer stem cells are a small, stem-like population within tumours, and
  inducing them to differentiate is a promising route to long-term remission.
  Here, a deep neural network trained on single-cell data from human
  pluripotent stem cells was used to screen thousands of drug-induced
  expression profiles from the LINCS database, with adversarial learning
  removing the bias between the two kinds of data. Five of the six top-ranked
  molecules tested in breast cancer cell lines proved effective, among them
  triptolide, OTS-167 and quinacrine. A collaboration with KAUST (Saudi
  Arabia) and CNR-IEOS Naples.
