headline: "MODAE: deep learning that bridges cell lines and patients"
description: >
  Drug screens on cancer cell lines drive much of drug development, but cell
  lines differ from real patient tumours. This paper presents MODAE, a deep
  learning model that integrates omics profiles from cell lines and patients
  and learns to predict drug sensitivity and patient survival at the same
  time. Keeping survival in the picture helps preserve the differences between
  patients that matter clinically, offering a new AI-based way to prioritize
  treatments for high-risk patients. Joint work with the University of Eastern
  Finland; the code is freely available on GitHub.
