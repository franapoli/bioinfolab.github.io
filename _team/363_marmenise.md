Maria Chiara obtained her Bachelor's Degree in Biotechnology with a thesis titled "Sviluppo del pensiero computazionale nello studio delle biotecnologie e delle STEM". Her work explores how computational thinking and basic logic can shape cognitive development and study paths in STEM disciplines and biotechnology, and how computer science and bioinformatics could be better integrated into Italian school and university curricula.
