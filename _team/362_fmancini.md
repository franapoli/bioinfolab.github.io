Fiorella is a Master's thesis student supervised by Prof.ssa Maria Teresa Canzoniero, with Prof. Luigi Cerulo as co-supervisor. Her thesis focuses on the RNA composition of extracellular vesicles, small membrane-bound particles released by cells that carry proteins, lipids and nucleic acids, including various RNA species, and act as vehicles for intercellular communication. By characterizing which RNAs are packaged into these vesicles, her work aims to shed light on their biological role and their potential as sources of circulating biomarkers.
