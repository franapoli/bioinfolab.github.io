Roberto obtained his Bachelor's Degree in Biotechnology after an internship and thesis in our lab, titled "Specializzazione di Large Language Models mediante LoRA: un'applicazione al dominio della bioinformatica". He fine-tuned Qwen2.5 models of increasing size (0.5B to 7B parameters) on a bioinformatics question-answering dataset using LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation), showing that all adapted models outperform their base versions, with the largest gains for mid-sized models, and that the whole process can run on a standard CPU-only desktop at negligible cost.
