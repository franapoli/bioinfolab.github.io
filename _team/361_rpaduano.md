Roberto is an intern and Bachelor's thesis student investigating the margin of improvement that parameter-efficient fine-tuning can bring to Large Language Models as their size varies. His project focuses on LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation), a technique that adapts a pre-trained model to new tasks by injecting small trainable low-rank matrices into its layers, rather than updating all of its parameters. By fine-tuning models of different sizes with LoRA and comparing their performance gains, Roberto aims to understand whether larger models benefit more, or less, from this lightweight adaptation strategy compared to smaller ones.
