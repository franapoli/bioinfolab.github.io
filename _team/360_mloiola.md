Mario is a Bachelor's student working on the structural analysis of the interactions between HPV viral proteins, p53, and the pep11 peptide, using molecular simulation and artificial intelligence tools. His thesis, titled "Analisi strutturale delle interazioni tra proteine virali HPV, p53 e pep11 mediante strumenti di simulazione e intelligenza artificiale", investigates how HPV oncoproteins interfere with the p53 tumor suppressor pathway, and explores the potential of the pep11 peptide to restore p53 function, combining structural bioinformatics techniques with AI-based modeling.
