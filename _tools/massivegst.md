A gene-set enrichment analysis tool based on the Mann–Whitney–Wilcoxon test, available as an R package on CRAN and as an online web service. Beyond p-values, it provides a Normalized Enrichment Score with a direct probabilistic interpretation, together with tabular and graphical results designed for reproducible research.
